## Outline (about 8 journal pages)

1. **Introduction.** Continuum models of SPAN cathodes (Simanjuntak et al. 2024); PINNs for battery models; what
   is hard here (three coupled reactions, a trace species over nine decades, precipitation threshold).
   Contributions: documented reference, sub-mV PINN, identifiability, inverse at the ideal-estimator level, two
   measured data sets with their limits, cost.
2. **Model** (short; equations in an appendix). Closures where the source is silent -> Table 1
   (PAPER_ASSUMPTIONS sections 2-3).
3. **Finite-volume reference and validation** -> Fig. 1 (`fig1_reference_vs_paper`): Fig. 6a 16 / 18 mV rms,
   Fig. 4b capacities.
4. **PINN formulation.** Hard extents e_m = exp(-T P_m), hard current projection, Delta phi by an implicit
   root solve, residual groups with adaptive weights, global salt and charge inventories, separator salt residual
   on its natural scale; training recipe (`configs/lispan_forward_final_*.json`).
5. **Forward accuracy and reproducibility** -> Fig. 2 (`fig2_forward_voltage`, three seeds per rate),
   Fig. 3 (`fig3_forward_species`), Table 2 (`T2_forward_accuracy`). Supplementary Fig. S1 / Table S1:
   benchmark choices (`figS1_assumption_checks`, `T1_assumption_checks`).
6. **What a discharge curve identifies** -> Fig. 4 (`fig4_identifiability`): one rate cannot separate
   kinetics from the OCV parameters and Z_CC; 0.1 C + 1 C bound all eight below ~1 %.
7. **Inverse problem on synthetic data** -> Fig. 5 (`fig5_inverse_synthetic`), Table 3 (`T3_inverse_synthetic`):
   Adam alone crawls along correlated directions; Levenberg-Marquardt on the parameters with frozen fields reaches
   the ideal estimator (FV least squares on the same noisy data) within its Cramer-Rao bound.
8. **Measured data** -> Fig. 6 (`fig6_measured`), Table 5 (`T5_measured`): (a) Simanjuntak Fig. 4b circles,
   FV and PINN fits at 0.1 + 1 C, predictions at 0.05 / 0.2 C, Z_CC recovered close to the paper's 0.035; (b)
   SPAN500 C/10 (Wang et al., Nat. Mater. 2026): a good single-rate calibration under two kinetic hypotheses that
   predict very different 1 C curves -> multi-rate data are required.
9. **Cost** -> Table 4 (`T4_cost`): an FV discharge takes seconds, PINN training hours; when a PINN is worth it
   (gradients for free in the inverse, mesh-free extension, amortised evaluation) and when it is not.
10. **Conclusions.**
